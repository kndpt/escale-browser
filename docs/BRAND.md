# Brand

What Escale looks like and is called: the name, the mark, the colours, the
type, the travel vocabulary and the kit of files drawn from the mark. The
interface is in [DESIGN](DESIGN.md); who may use the name and the mark is in
[TRADEMARKS](../TRADEMARKS.md).

## The name

*Escale* is French for a stopover: a port of call where you arrive and get
your bearings before going on. That is the product's job: you come back to a
project, recognise where you are (this Space, these tabs, this environment),
then act. Pronounced /ɛs.kal/. Written *Escale*, capital E, never all caps
except in small letter-spaced labels.

## The mark

![The mark](brand/mark.svg)

The stop, and the flight on from it. Read left to right, low to high:

- **the capsule** is the stop, where you are;
- **five dots** arc up and away from it, the trip;
- **the dot** is the next stop.

The dots sit so that every gap along the way, capsule to dot, dot to dot and
dot to stop, is the same.

**Construction.** One filled path on a 470 × 335 canvas, nothing outside it.
The path uses only absolute M, H, C and Z: the two readers in Swift parse
nothing else.

**Rules.**

- One colour, always: the ink of the surface it sits on. No outline, gradient,
  second colour or shadow on the mark itself; the Dock icon's shadow belongs
  to its plate.
- Never rotated, mirrored or re-spaced: the trip goes up and to the right.
- The bare mark is at least 24 px wide. Below that (a 16 px favicon), use it
  on a plate: the dots blur into a trail and the silhouette still reads.
- Leave the stop's radius (60 on the canvas) clear around it.

**With the name.** The mark comes first, then *Escale* set in the system sans
(SF Pro); in Settings › About, the mark is 32 pt tall beside the name in
medium 13.5 pt. There is no drawn wordmark. The large *Escale* in Bodoni Moda
on the site's masthead is a headline, not a logo.

## Colours

| Name | Value | Where |
|---|---|---|
| Espresso | `#221b16` | The site's ink; the plate behind the mark; the mark on light grounds |
| Cream | `#efe7dc` | The mark on espresso |
| Sand | `#e7ddd0` | The site's ground; social previews |
| Paper, Milk | `#f3eee6`, `#faf7f2` | The site's lighter surfaces |
| White, Ink | `#ffffff`, `#171717` | The Dock icon's plate and mark |

The app is neutral so pages keep their own colour: inside it the mark takes
`Palette.ink`, and the Dock icon is ink on white (see DESIGN). The warm
espresso, cream and sand are for everything around the app: the site,
favicons, avatars, previews. The DEV, STAGING and PROD colours belong to the
product's environment labels, not to the brand.

## Type

- **Site:** Bodoni Moda for display (SIL OFL, its optical size matched to the
  text size so hairlines stay visible), the system sans for text, the system
  mono for keys and addresses.
- **App:** the system font only, per DESIGN.

## The travel vocabulary

The stopover gives a small vocabulary that the product and the site reuse:
arrival, departure, boarding pass, gate, luggage tags, the departures board,
From / To, N°, and Bearings (getting your bearings when you arrive). In the
app it shapes Welcome's arrival and What's new's ticket.

- The metaphor frames a surface; it never replaces a plain label for an
  action. Menus keep the standard macOS names.
- One travel image per surface. A ticket does not also need a plane, a gate
  and a suitcase.
- Drawn aircraft appear only where a trip between two versions or two places
  is shown, like What's new's arc. The mark has none.

## The kit

`docs/brand/` holds every file drawn from the mark. It is generated by
`swift Icon/icon.swift --kit`, never edited by hand.

| File | For |
|---|---|
| `mark.svg`, `mark-cream.svg` | The bare mark, espresso or cream, as a vector |
| `png/mark-{256,512,1024}.png`, `png/mark-cream-…` | The same where SVG is refused; the number is the width |
| `icon.svg`, `png/icon-{16…1024}.png`, `AppIcon.icns` | The app icon, as in the Dock: press, README, store listings |
| `plate.svg`, `png/plate-{16…1024}.png`, `favicon.ico` | Favicons and small tiles: espresso, rounded 22.5% |
| `tile.svg`, `png/tile-{180,512,1024}.png` | Square plates for places that round their own corners (Apple's touch icon) |
| `png/avatar-800.png` | Profile pictures; the mark stays inside the circle they crop to |
| `png/social-1280x640.png` | A repository's social preview, uploaded by hand in its GitHub settings |

## Where the mark lives

The path is copied, not shared. These change together:

- `Sources/Escale/Design/Design.swift` (`Logomark`, drawn in Welcome's header
  and in Settings › About);
- `Icon/icon.swift` (the Dock icon `build.sh` draws, which the DMG also wears,
  and this kit);
- the website;
- pictures that show it, updated when they are retaken.

## Changing the mark

1. Edit the path and canvas in `Design.swift` and `Icon/icon.swift`.
2. Run `swift Icon/icon.swift --kit`, then check the kit's pictures at 16, 32
   and 512 px.
3. Check every place that sizes it: the app sizes it by height (Welcome,
   Settings › About), the site by width.
