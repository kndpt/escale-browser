---
highlight: The page at a phone's size
plain: See a site as a phone or tablet would, check where its layout changes, then open it on a real phone.
---
Developer mode's Screens (⌥⌘R) lays the page out at a phone's, a tablet's or any width under a ruler that shows the page's own CSS breakpoints; its edges drag and catch on each side of a breakpoint, elements sticking out past the sides are counted and outlined, and the page opens in the running Simulator or on a phone by QR code, with a warning when the dev server only listens on the Mac.
